# Reflection and feedback

You have finished the scenario questions. In this last section we would like to hear about your experience.

These questions ask how you went about making your decisions and what you thought of the information you were given. There are no right or wrong answers here. We are interested in your honest impressions, including anything you found confusing or unhelpful.
