# Thank you for taking part

This study is about **add-ons** — things you turn on or connect to give an AI assistant a new ability. For example, letting it check your email, browse the web, work with an app you already use, or follow a set of instructions you give it.

Different tools call these different things: skills, plugins, extensions, connectors, integrations, MCP servers, apps, custom agents or GPTs, workflows, scripts, or packages. You do not need to know the technical name for any of them. If you have ever clicked *connect*, *enable*, *install*, *turn on*, or *add* to give an AI assistant a new ability, that counts. We call them **add-ons** throughout.

Please answer from your own experience. There are no right or wrong answers, and we are not testing your technical knowledge or judging your decisions. We are interested in what you normally do — including the times you did not look into something further.
