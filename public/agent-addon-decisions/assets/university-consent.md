## What we'll ask

This survey is about how you decide which add-ons to use with AI assistants. It starts with a few short questions to confirm that this is a fit. If it is not, you will be told right away — within about a minute — and you will not be asked to continue. Because that takes almost no time, **there is no payment for that path**.

We will ask about one recent add-on decision, what you expect an add-on to be able to do, and, at the end, two made-up listings. Nothing can be installed or connected. One design detail about the listings is withheld until the last page.

There is no course credit. Taking part or not has no effect on your grades, standing, employment, funding, or any relationship with your university or this research team. We do not tell anyone who took part.

<div class="consent-callout"><strong>Please do not type</strong> passwords, keys, tokens, confidential code, or coursework you are not free to share.</div>

## The gift card

Everyone who finishes receives a **$10 e-gift card** by email. The last page asks for your name and university email. That information is stored separately from your answers, is used only to send the card, and is deleted once the card has been sent (within 30 days of you finishing, and not later than 90). You may skip it. Your answers still count.
