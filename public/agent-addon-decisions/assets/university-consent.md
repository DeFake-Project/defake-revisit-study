# Information sheet and consent — survey

**Study:** Understanding How People Use Add-Ons and Extensions with AI Assistants

**Protocol:** IRB0000378

**Investigators:** Ziming Zhao (Northeastern University) and Matthew Wright (Rochester Institute of Technology). Co-investigators Saniat Javid Sohrawardi (RIT) and Md Armanuzzaman (UTEP). Student researcher HaoRan Zhang (Northeastern).

This survey takes about 15–20 minutes. Students **18 or older** who have set up an add-on for an AI assistant in the past 6 months can take part. Everyone who finishes receives a **$10 e-gift card** by email. You can skip the page that asks where to send it. There is no course credit. Taking part or not has no effect on your grades, standing, employment, funding, or any relationship with your university or this research team. We do not tell anyone who took part.

The survey starts with a few short questions to confirm that this is a fit. If it is not, you will be told right away — within about a minute — and you will not be asked to continue. Because that takes almost no time, **there is no payment for that path**.

We will ask about one recent add-on decision, what you expect an add-on to be able to do, and, at the end, two made-up listings. Nothing can be installed or connected. One design detail about the listings is withheld until the last page.

If you want the gift card, the last page asks for your name and university email. That information is stored separately from your answers, is used only to send the card, and is deleted once the card has been sent (within 30 days of you finishing, and not later than 90). You may skip it. Your answers still count.

Please do not type passwords, keys, tokens, confidential code, or coursework you are not free to share.

Questions about the study: Saniat Javid Sohrawardi, john.sohrawardi@rit.edu. You may also contact Ziming Zhao (z.zhao@northeastern.edu) or Matthew Wright (matthew.wright@rit.edu).

Questions about your rights, or to raise a concern with someone independent of us: RIT Human Subjects Research Office, 141 Lomb Memorial Drive, Rochester, NY 14623 — hsro@rit.edu — 585-475-7673.

Selecting **I agree** confirms that you are 18 or older, have read this page, understand that an ineligible path is unpaid, understand that the gift card page is optional, and agree to take part.
