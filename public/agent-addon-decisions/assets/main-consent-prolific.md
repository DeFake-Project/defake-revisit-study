## What we'll ask

We will ask about one recent decision you made about an add-on, what you expect an add-on to be able to do, and what you would want to know before using one. Near the end you will read **two made-up add-on listings**. They are not real products. You will not install, connect, download, or run anything. One design detail about those listings is withheld until the final page. We told you that before you begin, and the final page explains it.

Thinking back over a past decision, you may find you would do it differently now. That is the main discomfort this survey might cause.

<div class="consent-callout"><strong>Please do not type</strong> passwords, keys, tokens, confidential code, or anything your employer would consider confidential.</div>

## Your data

We do not ask your name. Your Prolific ID is held only to pay you and to prevent duplicate participation, then deleted. Answers are stored in Firebase under an RIT institutional account in the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest. No analytics or advertising services run on them.

## If you are in the UK, the EEA, or Switzerland

Rochester Institute of Technology is the data controller. Our lawful basis is your explicit consent, given by ticking the box below, separate from agreeing to take part. You may withdraw that consent by emailing john.sohrawardi@rit.edu. Once your Prolific ID has been deleted, your record can no longer be identified as yours, including by us, so from that point we cannot act on a deletion request. Your data are transferred to the United States, which holds no UK or EU adequacy decision for this transfer. The transfer relies on your explicit consent (Article 49(1)(a) and the Swiss FADP). Your data will not be protected by UK or EU law, and those supervisory authorities have no jurisdiction over the recipient.
