# About this study

Thank you for taking part in this survey.

Everything in the two listings was fictional. The add-ons, authors, products, companies, and listings were made up for this study. They do not represent real products or services. There were no trick questions, and your answers were not judged as right or wrong.

A few details differed between people. Those are described below.

**The email and calendar listing.** Half of the people taking this survey saw a version that spelled out exactly what the add-on would be able to do — read your inbox, add events to one named calendar, not delete anything, expire after 30 days, not keep your message text, and check with you before sending anything. The other half saw a version whose whole description of what it wanted was one line: "Access your mailbox and calendar so InboxLane can help you." Everything else about the two was identical. You saw one of them, chosen at random.

**The two document listings.** These differed in whether the author carried a verification badge, how similar the two names and author names were, the install counts, the rating against how many people had rated it, when each was last updated, and whether the description was original or copied from the other. Which card appeared first was also varied.

We did not tell you which details we were changing, because saying so beforehand would have made you look for them.

**Your payment** is a **$10 e-gift card**, emailed within **7 days**. The next page asks where to send it. You can skip that page. Your answers still count, and skipping it means you have not given us your name or email.

**Want your data removed?** Write to john.sohrawardi@rit.edu within **30 days** of taking part. No explanation is needed. The name and email, if you provided them, are deleted once the card is sent, and not later than 90 days.

Survey answers are kept for 7 years after publication. Questions about your rights: RIT Human Subjects Research Office, hsro@rit.edu, 585-475-7673.
