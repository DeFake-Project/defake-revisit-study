# Information sheet and consent — screening questions

**Study:** Understanding How People Use Add-Ons and Extensions with AI Assistants

**Protocol:** IRB0000378

This is a few questions to see whether the main study is a fit for you. It takes about 2–4 minutes. If it is not a fit, you are paid **$0.55** and you are done. If it is a fit, you continue into the survey, which pays **$8.00**. You must be **18 or older**. You can stop at any time.

Researchers at Northeastern University, the Rochester Institute of Technology, and the University of Texas at El Paso are studying how people decide which add-ons to use with AI assistants — skills, plugins, extensions, MCP servers, connectors, custom agents, or workflows made by someone other than the company that built the assistant.

We will ask your age range, which AI tools you have used, what kinds of add-ons you have personally installed or connected, how recently, your general background, and how often you use these tools. One question asks you to describe in a sentence what your most recent add-on did.

**Please do not type passwords, keys, tokens, confidential code, or anything your employer would consider confidential.**

If the study is a fit, you continue straight into the main survey. The next page describes it in full before you begin, and you can stop there. If it is not a fit, you finish here and are paid $0.55. We are not testing you.

Your answers are stored under a randomly assigned code. We do not ask your name on this arm. We receive your Prolific ID to pay you and to avoid duplicate participation, and delete it from our dataset once that is done. Answers are stored in a Firebase database under a Rochester Institute of Technology institutional account in the United States. The data is encrypted in storage and in transit. Access requires an RIT account with multi-factor authentication and is limited to the five researchers on this project.

Taking part is your choice. Nothing happens if you decline, and it does not affect your standing on Prolific.

**About the study:** Saniat Javid Sohrawardi, john.sohrawardi@rit.edu. You may also contact Ziming Zhao at z.zhao@northeastern.edu or Matthew Wright at matthew.wright@rit.edu.

**If you are in the UK, the EEA, or Switzerland:** Rochester Institute of Technology is the data controller. The lawful basis is your explicit consent, which you give by ticking the separate box. You may withdraw it by emailing john.sohrawardi@rit.edu. Your data are transferred to the United States, which holds no UK or EU adequacy decision covering this transfer. The transfer relies on your explicit consent. The main survey's consent page sets this out in full.

Selecting **I agree** confirms that you are 18 or older, you have read this page, and you agree to answer these questions.
