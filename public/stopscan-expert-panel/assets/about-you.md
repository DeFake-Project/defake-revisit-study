# About you

Before we introduce STOP&SCAN, we would like to learn a little about your professional background.

Please answer the four short questions in the sidebar. They ask about:

- your main area of professional work;
- other related areas you work in;
- your approximate years of experience; and
- your familiarity with SIFT or lateral reading.

These questions help us understand how professional background may shape responses to the study. We do not ask for your name, institution, or job title.
